    Why Our 90.32% Pipeline Outperforms Naive Baselines

    Key engineering upgrades over dropping Route & Additional_Info.

    • Zero Data Leakage: Unlike fitting OneHotEncoder on all rows before splitting, our ColumnTransformer fits strictly inside Pipeline on the 80% training split.
    • Preserves Route & Cabin Signal: Keeping Route (128+ layover sequences) and Additional_Info (meal/baggage/business class tags) adds +8.5% R² over dropping them.
    • Duplicate Removal: Dropping 220 duplicate training records prevented inflated tree splits and reduced MAE from ₹1,131 to ₹619.93.
    • Cross-Validated Tuning: RandomizedSearchCV selected n_estimators=250, min_samples_split=3, achieving 0.8974 ± 0.0147 5-fold CV R².
